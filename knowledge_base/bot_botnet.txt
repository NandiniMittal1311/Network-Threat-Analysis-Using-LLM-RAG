TITLE: Botnet Command and Control Activity (MITRE ATT&CK T1071 / T1105)

A bot-infected host communicates periodically with a Command and Control (C2) server to receive
instructions or exfiltrate data. In CICIDS2017, the "Bot" label corresponds to traffic generated
by the ARES botnet malware running on infected victim machines.

Detection signals in flow data: periodic, regularly-timed connections to a small set of external
destination IPs/ports (low variance in Flow IAT Mean across repeated flows from the same host,
since malware beacons on a fixed interval), relatively small and consistent packet sizes for
beacon check-ins (low Packet Length Std), and occasional larger flows corresponding to payload
downloads or data exfiltration (spikes in Total Length of Fwd/Bwd Packets).

Why it is hard to catch with simple rules: botnet beacon traffic can closely resemble legitimate
periodic traffic (software update checks, heartbeat monitoring), so volume-based thresholds alone
produce many false positives or negatives. Behavioral/statistical modeling of timing regularity
and destination reputation is typically needed.

Mitigation: DNS/IP reputation blocklists for known C2 infrastructure, egress filtering to restrict
which external hosts internal machines may contact, and anomaly detection on beacon timing
regularity combined with destination rarity (host contacting an IP none of its peers contact).
